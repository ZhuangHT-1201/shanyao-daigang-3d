imagery.

## Overture Maps and East Asian Buildings

- Overture buildings release: `2026-10-21.0`
- Upstream dataset: East Asian Buildings
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8174931>
- Upstream licence: CC BY 4.0
- Authors named in the upstream record: Qian Shi, Jiajun Zhu, Zhengyu Liu,
  Haonan Guo, Mengxi Liu, Zihong Liu, Xiaoping Liu

The derived GeoJSON files in `public/data/` retain source metadata where
practical. Users should cite the upstream dataset when reusing them.

## Esri World Imagery

The project can load Esri World Imagery for local visual verification. Raw
satellite tiles are excluded from this repository by default. Provider terms
must be reviewed before public deployment, caching, or commercial use.

The screenshots under `screenshots/` are documentation of the current
prototype and may contain imagery or map content owned by their respective
providers. They are not relicensed under MIT.
